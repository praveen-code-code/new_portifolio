The Academic
Behind the Research

Professor Dr. Mohammed Ali Hussain received his Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering from Acharya Nagarjuna University, India. With over 17 years of immersive teaching and research experience across India and abroad.

"Awarded with the Mahatma Gandhi Award for Teaching & Research Excellence, Engg. & Tech. Research Excellence Award, Rashtriya Gaurav Award, and Best Computer Science Faculty Award."

His research spans cloud computing security, mobile authentication, machine learning, and network security — with international conference presentations in Malaysia and keynote speeches across India.
