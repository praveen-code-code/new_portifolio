About

The Academic
Behind the Research

Professor Dr. Mohammed Ali Hussain received his Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering from Acharya Nagarjuna University, India. He has more than 17 years of teaching experience in India and Abroad.

He has been awarded with different prestigious awards like Mahatma Gandhi Award for Teaching and Research Excellence, Engineering & Technology Research Excellence Award, Rashtriya Gaurav Award, Innovative Technological Research & Dedicated Teaching Professional Award and Best Computer Science Faculty Award from various Technical & Professional Organizations.

"Dr. Ali Hussain has published about 150 refereed technical articles in scholarly International Journals and proceedings of International Conferences. He has Published 6 Patents and 3 Book Chapters with reputed publishers."

He is Chief Editor/Advisory Board Member and Technical Review Member of more than 100 peer reviewed International Journals. He has acted as Program Committee Member for various IEEE Symposiums and conferences around the world.

He is a recognized supervisor for Ph.D. from various Universities and Produced 10 Ph.D's to his credit. He has been invited to deliver keynote speeches/Guest Lectures at various National & International Conferences in India and Abroad. He is a professional member of FISEEE, ASDF, IACSIT, IAENG, IAEST, IRACST and UACEE.
